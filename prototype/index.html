    How to play

    1. Everyone is a Civilian or a Criminal. Every revealed clue is true.
    2. Click a face-down tile and say what you think: Civilian or Criminal.
    3. Only answer when the clues prove it. No guessing.
    4. A correct answer flips the card and reveals its clue.
    5. Hover a clue to see which tiles it talks about. Stuck? Press Hint.
